# Challenge `cmp.M2` — Balanced Twin-Scale Loader

> [← Module `cmp` Overview](./overview.md) · **Format:** 🔥 Mastery Challenge (`cmp`) · **Environment:** [🚂 Switchyard](../../envs/switchyard.md)

Route incoming crates of varied weights (`1..25`) onto Left vs. Right docks while keeping both docks' **running total weights** balanced (`leftSum <= rightSum`), scrapping damaged crates (`~14–18 blocks`).
