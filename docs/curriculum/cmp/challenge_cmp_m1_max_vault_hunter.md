# Challenge `cmp.M1` — The Max-Vault Hunter

> [← Module `cmp` Overview](./overview.md) · **Format:** 🔥 Mastery Challenge (`cmp`) · **Environment:** [🤖 Robot Grid](../../envs/robot_grid.md)

Scan a multi-room vault of randomized floor dials to record the maximum value `m`, then fetch `m` energy cells to open the master exit (`~14–18 blocks`).
