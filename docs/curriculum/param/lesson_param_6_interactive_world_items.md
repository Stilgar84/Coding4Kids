# Lesson `param.6` — Interactive World Items (`Pick Up` & `Use/Drop`)

> [← Module `param` Overview](./overview.md) · **Format:** 📖 Explanation · **Environment:** [🤖 Robot Grid](../../envs/robot_grid.md)

Three bite-sized runnable demos contrasting the three distinct Grid item mechanics:
1. **🔑 Key** unlocking a color-matched gate 1 cell ahead (consumed on use).
2. **🪵 Plank** laid across water 1 cell ahead via `USE_DROP`, walked across, and **retrieved from 1 cell ahead** via `PICK_UP` so a single portable bridge can cross multiple rivers!
3. **📦 Crate** dropped onto (and retrievable from) the **current** floor tile/switch (with 1-slot backpack indicator).
