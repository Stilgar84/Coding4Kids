# Lesson `loop.8` — Meet the Depot (Stackable Rings & Blocks)

> [← Module `loop` Overview](./overview.md) · **Format:** 📖 Explanation · **Environment:** [🏗️ Depot](../../envs/depot.md)

Two runnable demos introducing physical LIFO stacks of colorful rings/blocks in the Depot:
1. `DEPOT_GRAB` / `DEPOT_PLACE` on Stack 1 — only the top ring/block glows while lower ones stay locked underneath.
2. `DEPOT_MOVE_TO` sliding the worker claw between Stacks and Output.
