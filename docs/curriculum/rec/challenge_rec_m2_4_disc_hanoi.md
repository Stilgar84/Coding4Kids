# Challenge `rec.M2` — 4-Disc Tower of Hanoi

> [← Module `rec` Overview](./overview.md) · **Format:** 🔥 Mastery Challenge (`rec`) · **Environment:** [🏗️ Depot](../../envs/depot.md)

Scale recursive `F1(●, ▲, ■)` in the Depot to transfer 4 sized rings (15 moves) across Stacks `1..3` without ever placing a larger ring onto a smaller one (`~14–18 blocks`).
